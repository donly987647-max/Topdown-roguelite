# P1 Five-minute Manual Test

Do not mark `P1-GATE-001` complete until this checklist is recorded.

## Controls

- [ ] WASD movement reacts immediately and diagonal speed does not exceed cardinal speed.
- [ ] Mouse aim remains independent while moving in all directions.
- [ ] Gamepad left/right sticks switch without input lock or drift.
- [ ] Space/B dash travels consistently and cannot be redirected mid-dash.
- [ ] R/X reload lasts approximately 1.15 seconds.

## Readability and fairness

- [ ] Every enemy shot has a visible line and audible pre-attack cue.
- [ ] The player can identify why each hit occurred.
- [ ] Dash invulnerability passes through bullets only during the intended interval.
- [ ] Precision dodge triggers once per projectile.
- [ ] Hazard endpoint correction prevents accidental dash entry in normal mode.

## Combat feel

- [ ] Service pistol cadence feels responsive, not automatic.
- [ ] General hits, strong feedback and player damage are distinguishable.
- [ ] Camera aim lead helps visibility without causing nausea.
- [ ] Screen shake remains readable and does not hide enemy bullets.
- [ ] Five minutes can be played without parser errors, softlocks or unexplained damage.
